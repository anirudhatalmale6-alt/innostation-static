Introduction

Innostation is the co-working space that helps (mainly foreign) SME’s and organizations land and develop in China. Our mission is to let all players in the eco-system benefit from what Suzhou has to offer and doing so, become a top-5 innovation hub in China.

Innostation is a not-for-profit organization, fully subsidized by the local governments; it is founded and managed by EU Sino, a European consulting firm.

We believe that a multicultural cooperation in a friendly environment serves the global innovation best.
