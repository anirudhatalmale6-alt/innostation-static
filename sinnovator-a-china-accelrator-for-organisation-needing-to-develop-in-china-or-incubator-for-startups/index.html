Accelerating you into China

If your organisation is looking for a partner to get you ready for China, then we’d like to present to you Sinnovator!

InnoStation is created and managed by EU Sino, a consulting company with specialized services for landing in China. Years of experience and a wide network allow us to develop a program or cooperation matching your needs.

Sinnovator has the ability to function as incubator for start-ups, partner for special acceleration programs or consultant to establish efficient joint ventures.
