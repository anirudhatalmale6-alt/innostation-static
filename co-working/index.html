why?
Affordability + Quality

Innostation at Suzhou North is truly a very attractive opportunity because of it’s proximity to Shanghai, with high standard of living, while not far from modern manufacturing zones.
With a small budget you get all the advantages of the new China!

Suzhou, some facts
  • 7th largest GDP
  • #1 center for Industry 4.0
  • Top level universities of Jiangsu province
  • preferred living place of expat families working in Shanghai
  • fin out more here: Suzhou-XiangCheng-HSR
Suzhou-North Station

This bullet-train station (right in front of Innostation) puts us only 25 min  from downtown Shanghai.

Enormous investments in new-technologies like robotics and AI, with crown jewel a new state of the art village being built North of the Station (more info : “link to page suzhou-xiangcheng-hsnrt#newvillage”)

Affordable

As real-estate and HR costs become more andmore a challenge, in Suzhou this is still affordable: new buildings and well educated staff is already in Suzhou or is happy to move here.

Innovation Ecosystem

Although innovation was already thriving, and 2 large Chinese incbuators were already developing the start-up scene; there was still a lack of young foreign enterprises landing in Suzhou. HSRNT with InnoStation is the answer to that. It’s our mission to build a multicultural eco-system and become a top-5 innovation hub in China.
