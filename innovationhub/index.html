Start-Up & Innovation HUB

Our vision is to create in Suzhou the 4th startup- and Innovation HUB of China, after the top 3: Shanghai, Shenzhen and Beijing.

Such Ecosystem consists of a collaborative mix of service suppliers, entrepreneurs, financiers, governmental offices, corporate programs, etc…
Innostation is internationalizing the existing community by interweaving foreign actors locally and developing cooperation with foreign organizations. Via coordination we aim to continuously improve the ecosystem to become more efficient for all.

Read further to see how you can be part of it and then contact us to discuss further!

02

The Concept

Attractive but affordable location, a co-working space that is run by foreign China experts who (and that's unique) have been hired by the local government to develop internationally a new district.
  1. The infrastructure has all one can expect from a modern office, see: co-working.
  2. Similar projects abroad: Station F (Paris), B.Amsterdam, Technoport (Luxemburg), … with which we’re setting up cooperation.
  3. Players in the eco-system: entrepreneurs (startup or SME), public administration (local government and support offices), financiers (investors, VC, banks, …), programs (corporate innovation, mentorship, …), foreign development agencies, universities, …
  4. Connecting needs with supplies, all under one roof.
    We coordinate to give all players of the community best business development opportunities and guidance.

why?
Affordability + Quality

Innostation at Suzhou North Station is truly a very attractive opportunity because of it’s proximity to Shanghai (25 min.), with high standard of living, while not far from modern manufacturing zones.
With a small budget you get all the advantages of the new China!

Yangtze River Delta

The megalopolis in the making around Shanghai, a futuristic perfect world and business heart of Asia.

The transport infrastructure (air-land-water) in the YRD Economic Zone is highly developed​.

Suzhou, some facts
  • 7th largest GDP
  • #1 center for Industry 4.0
  • Top level universities of Jiangsu province
  • preferred living place of expat families working in Shanghai
  • fin out more here: Suzhou-XiangCheng-HSR
Suzhou-North Station

This bullet-train station (right in front of Innostation) puts us only 25 min  from downtown Shanghai.

Enormous investments in new-technologies like robotics and AI, with crown jewel a new state of the art village being built North of the Station (more info : “link to page suzhou-xiangcheng-hsnrt#newvillage”)

Affordable

As real-estate and HR costs become more and more a challenge, in Suzhou this is still affordable: new buildings and well educated staff is already in Suzhou or is happy to move here.

Innovation Ecosystem

Although innovation was already thriving, and 2 large Chinese incbuators were already developing the start-up scene; there was still a lack of young foreign enterprises landing in Suzhou. HSRNT with InnoStation is the answer to that. It’s our mission to build a multicultural eco-system and become a top-5 innovation hub in China.
